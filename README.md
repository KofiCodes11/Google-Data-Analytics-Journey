### Module 1 Reflection: Learning to Think Like an Analyst
I have successfully completed Module 1 and mastered the core foundational concepts of the data ecosystem:

* **Data Ecosystems:** Understood how data, software tools, and human decisions interact to drive business strategies.
* **Analytics vs. Data Science:** Learned to differentiate between Data Analytics (analyzing existing data to answer specific business questions) and Data Science (creating new algorithms, models, and predictive tools).
* **The 6 Phases of the Data Life Cycle:** 
  1. **Ask:** Defining the problem and understanding stakeholder expectations.
  2. **Prepare:** Identifying, locating, and gathering relevant data sources.
  3. **Process:** Cleaning the data to eliminate errors, inaccuracies, or duplicates.
  4. **Analyze:** Finding patterns, trends, and relationships within the data.
  5. **Share:** Visualizing findings and presenting them clearly to an audience.
  6. **Act:** Utilizing the insights to make final data-driven business decisions.
